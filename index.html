Questions

Before you order

Do I really pay on delivery?

Yes. You don't pay anything when you place your order. You pay when the furniture reaches you.

Are the products new?

Every item is brand new and imported. The photos and videos on this page are of the actual products, not stock images.

How much is delivery, and how long does it take?

It depends on where you are. Once you send your order, we'll confirm the delivery cost and date before anything is dispatched.

Does it come assembled?

Some pieces need simple assembly when they arrive. If you want help putting yours together, mention it in your order and we'll let you know what we can arrange in your area.

Can I see more photos or videos first?

Of course. Message us about any item and we'll send more pictures or a video.
