Good questions

Anything else, message us. A real person replies.

Do I really pay on delivery?

Yes. Placing an order costs nothing. You pay when the furniture reaches you and you're happy with it.

Are the products new?

Every item is brand new and imported. The photos and videos here are of the actual products, not stock images.

How much is delivery?

Nothing. Delivery is free on every order. The price you see is the price you pay.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on your location. After you send your order, we confirm the delivery date before anything is dispatched.

Some pieces need simple assembly. If you'd like help putting yours together, mention it in your order and we'll tell you what we can arrange in your area.

Of course. Message us about any item and we'll send extra pictures or a quick video.
